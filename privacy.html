Moner Privacy Policy

Effective: 2 November 2026

Moner ("the app") is a personal spending tracker for Android. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. In short: your spending data stays on your phone. Moner has no server and no account, and the developer never receives your data.

Data stored on your phone

  • Payments you record: amount, category, date and time, payment method, merchant and notes.
  • Receipts found in Gmail (only if you connect Gmail, see below): merchant, amount, date, payment method and reference number.
  • Profile: the name and optional photo you set, and your monthly limit and settings.

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is not sent to the developer or any server run by the developer. Android may include it in your device's own Google backup if device backup is on; you can turn that off in Android settings. Uninstalling the app or using "Clear all data" in Settings deletes it.

Gmail (optional)

If you choose "Connect Gmail", Moner asks Google for read-only access to your Gmail (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly). Moner uses it only to:

  • search your mailbox for payment receipts from banks, e-wallets, QRIS and ride or delivery services, and
  • read those receipt emails on your phone to fill in the amount, merchant, date and payment method.

Moner does not send, delete, modify or label any email. Email content is processed only on your device. It is never sent to the developer, never sent to any AI service, and never shared with or sold to anyone. Only the receipt details listed above are kept, in the app on your phone. You can disconnect Gmail at any time in Moner's Settings, which revokes the access at Google; you can also remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Moner's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Camera and photos

Moner uses the camera, or a photo you pick from your gallery, only when you scan a receipt or split a bill. By default the photo is read on your phone (Google ML Kit, on-device) and is not kept after reading.

AI receipt reading (optional, off by default)

If you turn on "Read receipts with AI" in Settings (you are asked to agree first), the photo of a receipt you scan is sent to Google's Gemini model through Google Firebase AI Logic so it can be read more accurately. Only that photo is sent: no Gmail content, no payment history and no profile data. Google processes it under the Gemini API Additional Terms and the Google Privacy Policy; on Gemini's free tier Google may use what is sent to improve its products, so avoid scanning receipts that show card numbers or personal details. You can turn AI reading off at any time; scans then stay on your phone.

App integrity

To stop others misusing the AI service, the app uses Google Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity, which sends a device and app integrity check to Google when AI reading is used. It does not include your spending or Gmail data.

What Moner does not do

  • No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data, and no use of it for advertising or credit decisions.
  • No account with the developer.

Children

Moner is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
