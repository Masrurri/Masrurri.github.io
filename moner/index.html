Moner

Spending tracker for Android

Know where your money went, without typing it all in.

A calm record of what you spend each month. Add a payment in seconds, keep the ones you repeat as quick actions, bring in bank and e-wallet receipts from Gmail, scan a paper one, or split a bill with friends.

How it works

01Set a monthly limit

One number, typed on the keypad. Home shows what's left as you spend.

02Add a spend in seconds

Tap Spend: what it was for, the amount, a category. Slide to save.

03Keep the ones you repeat

Tick Save as a quick action. Next time it's one tap from Home.

04Let receipts come to you

Connect Gmail and Moner finds payment receipts from banks, e-wallets, QRIS and ride apps. Review them, then record one or Record All.

05Split the bill

Scan the receipt, tap who had what, and share each person's part as an image.

06See where it went

Insights groups the month by category and place.

Privacy and safety

Everything stays on this phone

No account and no server. Nothing is uploaded, except a receipt photo when you turn on AI reading.

Stored only on your phone

Payments, limits, quick actions and notes live in Moner's storage on the device. Uninstalling removes them.

Gmail is read-only, no AI

Payment receipts are read on your phone. Moner never sends, changes or deletes mail.

Scans are read on your phone

Photos aren't kept, only the numbers, unless you turn on AI reading.

AI reading is your choice

Off until you turn it on. Then only Scan receipt and Split bill send the photo to Google Gemini.

You can take it with you

Export a CSV, or delete everything in one tap from Settings.
