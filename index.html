Moner

A personal spending tracker for Android.

Record what you spend, set a monthly limit and see what's left. Moner can also find payment receipts from banks, e-wallets and QRIS in your Gmail (read-only, processed on your phone), scan printed receipts with the camera, and split a bill between friends. Your data stays on your phone: there is no account and no server.
